Buy or rent?¹

The calculator compares what buying and renting an apartment or a car will cost over your chosen period. Buying includes not only the price but also running costs, loss of value and the income the money could have earned on deposit.

A simplified calculation. The purchase is assumed to be made with your own money, without a loan. Income forgone is what the money spent on the purchase would have earned at the chosen rate with compounding. Inflation, exchange rates, income from renting the property out and tax on the return on spare money are not included. A foreigner cannot own land in Thailand: for property the calculation applies to a condominium apartment.

¹ How the calculator works. It compares the full cost of the two options over your chosen period. Buying = price with purchase costs + running costs for all the years + income forgone (what this money would have earned at your chosen rate with annual compounding) − the amount you receive on sale at the end of the period (the price adjusted for annual growth or loss of value, minus selling costs). Renting = the sum of rent payments for all the years, including annual rent growth. The cheaper option is the one with the smaller total; "per month" is the total divided by the number of months in the period.
