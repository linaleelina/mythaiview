A simplified calculation: it does not take into account limits on individual deductions, credit for foreign tax or special regimes (LTR 17%). For your return, use the data on rd.go.th.

⁶ The effective tax rate is an economic term: the actual share of tax in income, tax for the year ÷ income for the year. The income tax scale is progressive and part of the income is reduced by expenses and deductions, so the actual rate is always lower than the rate of the top band your income falls into.

⁷ How the calculator works. 1) Expenses are deducted from income: for salary, 50% but no more than 100,000 baht; for other income, nothing. 2) Allowances are deducted: personal 60,000 baht, spouse without income 60,000, 30,000 for each child, Social Security Fund contributions and any other allowances you entered. 3) The progressive 2026 scale is applied to the remainder — taxable income: each part of income is taxed at the rate of its band (up to 150,000 baht — 0%, 150,001 to 300,000 — 5%, and so on up to 35%), and the tax for each band is added up. 4) Tax per month is the annual tax divided by 12; the effective rate is tax divided by income.
